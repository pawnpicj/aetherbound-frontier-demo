# Kael — character redesign v2

Created with the built-in Imagegen tool, followed by an Imagegen background-removal edit. Six illustrated key poses, timed in Phaser with motion tweens and hit effects; this is a pose-based prototype, not a full frame-by-frame animation production set.

Original generation prompt:

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: production 2D RPG character pose atlas, transparent PNG. Create ONE landscape sprite sheet with EXACTLY 6 separate full-body drawings of the SAME original character, Kael the Ashen Knight, arranged in a strict 3-column by 2-row grid of equal square cells (overall 3:2 aspect ratio). Each cell has generous transparent padding on every side; no drawing overlaps another cell. Hand-painted anime fantasy chibi, 3-heads-tall proportions, beautiful confident expressive face, tousled dark crimson hair with copper tips, dark charcoal steel plate armor with engraved warm gold trim, asymmetric large shoulder armor, torn deep red flowing cape, thick armored boots, one large broad silver greatsword with glowing amber fissures. Warm skin and amber eyes. Rich material shading, clean dark outline, restrained tiny detailing readable at 140px tall. All six drawings must show identical costume, face, sword and proportions. Three-quarter battle view facing RIGHT, camera angle and scale consistent, feet baseline at 86% down each cell for upright poses. Row 1 col 1: relaxed alert idle stance, sword angled upward at his right side, full silhouette contained. Row 1 col 2: anticipation, crouched and greatsword pulled back ready to strike. Row 1 col 3: powerful forward horizontal sword slash, cape following motion, no giant external effect. Row 2 col 1: Brave Burst heroic raised-sword overhead pose, small orange flame hugging blade only. Row 2 col 2: taking a hit, recoiling with sword defensively across torso, face determined. Row 2 col 3: knocked down resting on one knee with sword on ground, exhausted not dead. The character should occupy about 72% of each square cell height. True transparent alpha background, NO ground, NO cast shadows, NO checkerboard pattern, NO frames, NO grid lines, NO labels, NO text, NO watermarks. This is actual art to be loaded by a Phaser game, not a screenshot of a sprite sheet.

Background-removal pass: preserve poses and costume, remove painted surroundings to RGBA transparency. Final source retained as kael-transparent-source-v2.png. The preparation script only slices, resizes and packs the generated art; alpha is preserved.

Runtime assets: public/assets/kael/ (WebP atlas, six pose cutouts, idle image and cropped portrait). New design can be compared with the original in the character showcase.
